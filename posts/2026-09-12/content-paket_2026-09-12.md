## 3. Reddit-Play — r/Parenting (MicroQuest, value-first, EN, OHNE Link im Post)

**Titel:** The Saturday screen-time guilt hit again — so I stopped counting minutes and started asking two questions instead

**Post:**
> Rainy Saturday here, tablet came out before 9am, and the familiar guilt arrived right on cue. I went down a rabbit hole on this recently and the research take that stuck with me: screen-time guilt is almost universal among parents — and it's mostly aimed at the wrong target. The minute count matters less than two other questions.
>
> 1. **What is the screen replacing right now?** Sleep, outdoor play, or a rainy morning where the alternative was me doomscrolling next to them? Very different answers.
> 2. **What is the screen giving back?** Passive autoplay feeds vs. something my kid comes away from wanting to tell me about — those are not the same category, even at the same minute count.
>
> Since I started asking those two instead of watching the clock, weekends got calmer. Some Saturdays the answer is still "too much, go get your boots". But the blanket guilt is gone.
>
> Curious how others handle it: do you do minute limits, content rules, or something else entirely?
