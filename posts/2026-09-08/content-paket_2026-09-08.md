That's seven small decisions a week instead of one, and they collide with real life — early flights, kids, coffee on autopilot.
>
> What's working so far: anchoring it to something that happens every morning anyway (alarm off → pill → shower, eat after), and actually logging the moment I take it instead of trusting memory, because "did I already take it today?" hits different when doubling up isn't an option.
>
> Curious what systems others here have landed on — especially people who travel or work shifts.
